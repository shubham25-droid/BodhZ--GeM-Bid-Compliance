# 🇮🇳 Smart India Hackathon 2026 — Problem Statement SIH26100

## Problem Statement Details
- **PS ID**: `SIH26100`
- **Title**: AI-Powered Integrated Bid Compliance Verification Platform for GeM Procurement
- **Category**: Software
- **Domain Bucket**: Smart Governance & Public Procurement
- **Target Organization**: Ministry of Commerce & Industry / Government e-Marketplace (GeM) & State Directorates (DTE Maharashtra)

---

## 🎯 Official Scope & Challenge
Public procurement officers evaluate thousands of technical proposals submitted by vendors across central and state portals. Key operational bottlenecks include:
1. **Voluminous PDF Bundles**: Bidders submit hundreds of unindexed pages containing balance sheets, certificates, test reports, and compliance undertakings.
2. **Fragmented Government Registries**: Officers must manually cross-verify details across GSTN, MCA21, EPFO, ESIC, DigiLocker, and blacklists.
3. **Overlooked Discrepancies**: Name variations, expired certifications, invalid UDINs, and non-compliant OEM authorizations.
4. **Vigilance & CAG Auditability**: The necessity of maintaining an immutable cryptographic audit log for post-procurement scrutiny.

---

## 🛡️ Non-Negotiable Constraint
> **BodhZ is a decision-support and verification platform. The AI does not independently qualify or disqualify bidders. Final qualification/disqualification authority remains 100% with the authorized Human Procurement Officer under GFR 2017 Rule 144.**
