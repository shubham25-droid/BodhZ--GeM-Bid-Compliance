BodhZ Architecture and SIH26100 Workflow

Problem Statement ID: SIH26100
Title: AI-Powered Integrated Bid Compliance Verification Platform for GeM Procurement

Core Workflow:
1. Procurement Officer authenticates and accesses portal.
2. Officer creates or selects an active GeM Tender workspace.
3. Tender specification PDF uploaded; requirements and weights extracted.
4. Bidder submission files ingested and processed via OCR.
5. 13 Verification Gateway Nodes queried for cross-document consistency.
6. Compliance score (0-100) and Risk Tier computed.
7. Grounded findings and recommendations generated with evidence page citations.
8. Procurement Officer reviews findings and records final qualification/disqualification decision.
9. Immutable audit trail hash generated and official PDF audit report stored.
