Compliance Score and Risk Tier Rules

A Compliance Score is an automated metric ranging from 0 to 100 that indicates how thoroughly a bidder satisfies tender eligibility criteria, statutory rules, and document verifications.

Scoring Factors:
1. Active statutory registrations (GSTN, Udyam MSME, PAN)
2. Valid OEM Authorization Letters
3. Document consistency and OCR extraction accuracy
4. Absence of debarment or blacklisting records

Risk Tiers:
LOW RISK (80-100): Fully compliant with zero critical mismatches.
MEDIUM RISK (60-79): Minor discrepancies like spelling variations. Officer review recommended.
CRITICAL RISK (<60): Major non-compliance such as expired OEM letter or blacklisting.

Human Decision Authority:
The Compliance Score and Risk Tier are strictly decision-support metrics. The 100% final qualification or disqualification decision remains with the Procurement Officer.
