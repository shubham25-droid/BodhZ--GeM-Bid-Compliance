Public Procurement Bid Definition

In public procurement under GeM and Central Public Procurement Portal guidelines, a Bid represents a formal, legally binding technical and commercial offer submitted by an eligible supplier or OEM in response to an official tender.

Core Components of a Bid Submission:
1. Technical Bid: Specification compliance sheets, experience certificates, and OEM Manufacturer Authorization Forms.
2. Financial Bid: Price schedule and BOQ rate breakdowns.
3. Statutory Eligibility Instruments: GST Registration, PAN, Udyam MSME Certificate, and EMD or exemption undertakings under GFR 2017 Rule 170.

Difference between Bid and Tender:
A Tender is the public invitation issued by a government department inviting offers.
A Bid is the actual offer submitted by a vendor in response to that tender.
